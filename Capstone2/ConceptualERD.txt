Conceptual Entity-Relationship Diagram
=======================================
Physical Activity and Chronic Disease Burden Across Europe
Capstone 2 — Conceptual Data Model

Entities:

1. Country          — A European country represented in the dataset
2. AgeGroup         — A standardized age bracket
3. Sex              — Biological sex category
4. HealthIndicator  — A specific measured health indicator
5. IndicatorCategory— Broad category grouping health indicators
6. SurveyWave       — A specific EHIS survey round
7. Measurement      — Central fact: one recorded value for a country/age/sex/indicator/wave
8. DataSource       — Original Eurostat dataset provenance

Relationships:

[Country]  --1---------M-- [Measurement]   : a country has many measurements
[AgeGroup] --1---------M-- [Measurement]   : an age group appears in many measurements
[Sex]      --1---------M-- [Measurement]   : a sex category appears in many measurements
[HealthIndicator] --1---M-- [Measurement]  : an indicator appears in many measurements
[SurveyWave]  --1---------M-- [Measurement]: a survey wave has many measurements
[DataSource]  --1---------M-- [HealthIndicator]: a data source provides many indicators
[IndicatorCategory] --1---M-- [HealthIndicator]: a category groups many indicators

Star Schema Structure
======================

Central Fact Table: Measurement

Dimension Tables:
  - Country
  - AgeGroup
  - Sex
  - HealthIndicator
  - IndicatorCategory
  - SurveyWave
  - DataSource

This constitutes a star schema because Measurement sits at the center with
all other entities radiating outward as dimensions. Each dimension table
contains descriptive attributes (e.g., Country has country_name and region;
AgeGroup has lower_bound and upper_bound). The Measurement fact table
contains the quantitative value plus foreign keys referencing each dimension.
No dimension table references another dimension table as a foreign key,
preserving the star (not snowflake) structure.

IndicatorCategory and DataSource relate to HealthIndicator rather than
directly to Measurement, forming a slight snowflake extension. This is
acceptable because IndicatorCategory changes rarely and DataSource is
one-per-source, keeping join depth to a maximum of two hops from the
fact table.
