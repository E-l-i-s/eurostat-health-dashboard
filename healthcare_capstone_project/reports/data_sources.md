# Data Source Identification

## Dataset 1: Renewable Energy Trends
1. **Our World in Data (OWID) Energy Dataset:** A comprehensive,-global dataset providing time-series data on renewable energy sources.
2. **Official Source:** [Our World in Data](https://ourworldindata.org/energy)
3. **Relevance:** Contains high-granularity data for Austria, including solar, wind, hydro, and biomass consumption.

## Dataset 2: Fossil Fuel and Carbon Emissions
1. **Our World in Data (OWID) Energy Dataset:** Provides detailed fossil fuel consumption (coal, oil, gas) and carbon intensity metrics.
2. **Official Source:** [Our World in Data](https://ourworldindata.org/energy)
3. **Relevance:** Enables longitudinal analysis of carbon intensity and fossil fuel dependency in Austria.
