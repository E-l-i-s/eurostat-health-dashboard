# Case Study Selection Report

## Case Study 1: The Rise of Renewables in Austria
**Importance:** As part of the EU Green Deal, tracking the transition to renewable energy (wind, solar, hydro) is critical for monitoring climate goals.
**Key Question:** How has the share of renewable energy in Austria's total energy consumption changed over the last two decades?

## Case Study 2: Fossil Fuel Decarbonization in Austria
**Importance:** Monitoring the reduction of coal, oil, and gas usage is essential for assessing the progress of Austria's decarbonization strategy.
**Key Question:** What are the trends in fossil fuel consumption and carbon intensity in Austria, and how do they correlate with renewable growth?
