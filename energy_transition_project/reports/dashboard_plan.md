# Dashboard Plan

## Dashboard 1: Strategic Operations Overview (Strategic)
- **Target Users:** Hospital Executives, Health Department Administrators.
- **Goal:** Monitor high-level operational efficiency and patient demographic trends.
- **Story:** "How is our hospital capacity managed and who are our primary patient groups?"
- **Visualizations:**
    - **Quantitative:**
        1. Total Patient Population (KPI Card)
        2. Average Length of Stay (KPI Card)
        3. Stay Duration by Admission Type (Bar Chart)
        4. Patient Volume per Hospital (Donut Chart)
    - **Qualitative:**
        1. Gender Demographic Breakdown (Pie Chart)
        2. Top 5 Clinical Diagnoses (Horizontal Bar Chart)
        3. Admission Type Distribution (Table)

## Dashboard 2: Clinical Risk & Correlation Analysis (Analytical)
- **Target Users:** Medical Researchers, Clinical Quality Teams.
- **Goal:** Uncover correlations between patient risk factors and clinical outcomes.
- **Story:** "What lifestyle and demographic factors drive longer hospital stays and higher risk profiles?"
- **Visualizations:**
    - **Quantitative:**
        1. BMI vs. Length of Stay (Scatter Plot)
        2. Patient Age vs. Length of Stay (Scatter Plot)
        3. Average Stay by Gender (Bar Chart)
        4. Risk Factor Prevalence (Horizontal Bar Chart)
    - **Qualitative:**
        1. High-Risk Patient Segment (Table)
        2. Risk Factor Category Overview (TreeMap/Bar)
        3. Gender-based Outcome Distribution (Donut Chart)
