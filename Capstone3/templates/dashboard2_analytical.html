Analytical Deep-Dive - Renewable Technology Growth, Intensity, and Energy Flows
Wind generation grew from 0.1 TWh in 2000 to 8.9 TWh in 2024 - an 89-fold increase driven by the 2002 Ökostromergesetz feed-in tariff and 2021 Renewable Expansion Act (EAG). Solar reached 8.8 TWh in 2024, effectively zero a decade prior. Despite this growth, hydro remains the structural anchor at 40–46 TWh annually, constrained by geography rather than policy ambition. Energy intensity has declined in every decade since 1970, decoupling GDP growth from primary energy demand.
