Strategic Overview - Austria Energy Transition, 1900–2024
Austria has reached a 42% renewable share of gross final energy (Eurostat SDG_07_40, 2024 estimate) - 16 percentage points above the EU27 average and more than double the 1970 level. CO₂ emissions peaked at 80.4 Mt in 2005 and have declined 29% since. Nuclear power has been constitutionally prohibited since the 1978 Zwentendorf referendum; Austria generates no nuclear electricity.
